The Core Problem

Campus Energy Inefficiency

Educational and corporate campuses routinely face severe visibility gaps:

  • Opaque consumption: Unknown which academic blocks consume disproportionate power.
  • Off-hours wastage: Air-conditioning, computer labs, and lighting left running overnight.
  • Curtailed solar: Inability to synchronize high daytime solar generation with flexible loads.
  • Unpredictable demand peaks: Exorbitant grid demand charges during simultaneous load spikes.

The WattWise Solution

End-to-End AI Engine

WattWise unifies telemetry, analytics, and dynamic machine learning into a single actionable command center:

  • Telemetry & Monitoring: Real-time tracking of consumption, solar yield, and battery reserves.
  • Isolation Forest Anomaly Detection: Instant detection of overnight spikes and battery drops.
  • Random Forest Demand Forecasting: 24-hour predictive planning to avoid grid tariff peaks.
  • Dynamic Recommendations: Targeted operational policies to shift loads and maximize solar ROI.
